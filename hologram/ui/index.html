On opening this site in its own visible tab, JARVIS requests the microphone once. Allow it in the browser. If your browser requires a gesture, tap the core or press Space. Browser voice input uses its own recognition service. Microphone use and transcription are subject to permission and network availability. Say listen patiently for longer pauses, or what did you hear for the last phrase. Say fast voice on for a quicker device voice, or fast voice off to try the natural voice again. If a voice request cannot be matched, try again; say open chat to review the terms for optional open conversation.
